how to play

WASD/arrows or hold the mouse to steer. J/space toggles joinable (touching joinable bodies merge into one group with a shared pool). Mines fill the pool; touching a member's home pad banks it to them. Hold L to leave a group with a share of the pool. 1-4 declare which resource you are after. First to fill all four needs wins.
